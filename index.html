About the game

Short matches, big plays

Brawl Stars is a mobile game where you control a brawler in short online matches against other players. Every brawler has a main attack and a Super.

Play with friends or solo, unlock new brawlers and climb the trophy road.

Pick your fight

Game Modes

3v3

Gem Grab

Collect gems from the mine in the middle of the map. The first team to hold 10 gems until the countdown ends wins.

Solo / Duo

Showdown

Battle royale where 10 players, solo or in pairs, fight to be the last one standing in a shrinking arena.

3v3

Brawl Ball

Two teams compete to score two goals by carrying or shooting the ball into the opponent's net.

3v3

Heist

A fast-paced mode built around base defense and destruction: break the enemy safe before they break yours.

3v3

Bounty

Defeat enemies to collect their stars. The team with the most stars when time runs out wins.

3v3

Knockout

No respawns. Knock out the whole enemy team to win the round, and win two rounds to take the match.
